Delete Your Account

If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated personal data from our platform, please submit a request using the contact information below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to youssifahmed@dev.com.
  2. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address or phone number associated with your account and name of the app.

What will be deleted?

  • Your account information.
  • Your profile data.
  • Your course progress and exam history.
  • Any personal information associated with your account.

Data retention

Your account and associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days of verifying your request.

Certain information may be retained if required by applicable laws or legal obligations.
